How a script is generated
  1. 1
    Pick the classChoose a program, module and class.
  2. 2
    Approve the outlineCards and timings come from the class agenda and learning outcomes.
  3. 3
    Script is written card by cardEach card gets its code, quiz, diagram or animation.
  4. 4
    Review and fixThe script is checked against the rulebook and issues are fixed.
  5. 5
    Approve and exportThe module owner approves. Download as Word, PDF or HTML.
